SOP-09: Quarantine, Disposition and Customer Notification
Owner: Quality | Version 2.2

§1 Quarantine
A quarantined load must not be delivered or sold. The driver keeps it sealed and the dispatcher arranges transfer to a quarantine area or a partner cold-store.

§2 Disposition
Only the Quality team decides whether a quarantined load is released, reworked or destroyed. Dispatchers and drivers must not make this decision.

§3 Customer notification
The customer is notified within 2 hours of a quarantine decision. The notice states the cargo, the trip ID, the time of the excursion and the next steps.

§4 Records
The trip outcome (fine or spoiled) is recorded by Quality after the disposition decision.
