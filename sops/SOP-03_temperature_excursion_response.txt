SOP-03: Temperature Excursion Response
Owner: Quality | Version 2.4

§1 Upper temperature limits
An excursion is cargo-area temperature above the upper limit for that cargo. Upper limits are: vaccine 8°C, dairy 6°C, meat 6°C, seafood 4°C, produce 10°C.

§2 Response steps
Step 1: verify the reading is real using SOP-07. Step 2: call the driver and check the reefer unit and the door. Step 3: record the start time, peak temperature and duration. Step 4: if the temperature stays above the limit, apply SOP-05 when the reefer is at fault.

§3 Time thresholds
An excursion lasting 15 minutes or more is escalated to Level 2 under SOP-06. Cumulative time above the limit of 30 minutes or more means the load is placed in quarantine and escalated to Level 3.

§4 Documentation
Export the sensor log for the trip and attach it to the incident ticket before the end of the shift.
