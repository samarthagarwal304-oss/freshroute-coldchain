SOP-08: Power and Battery Management
Owner: Fleet Maintenance | Version 1.4

§1 Battery warnings
A sensor battery below 20% raises a warning for the dispatcher. A battery below 10% means the driver must plug the sensor into vehicle power.

§2 Loss of power
If the sensor loses power, the driver records the cargo temperature manually every 15 minutes and reports it to the dispatcher.

§3 Charging
Sensor units are charged at the depot between trips. A unit returned with less than 30% battery is flagged to Fleet Maintenance.
