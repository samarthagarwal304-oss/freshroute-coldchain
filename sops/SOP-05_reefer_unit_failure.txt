SOP-05: Refrigeration (Reefer) Unit Failure
Owner: Fleet Maintenance | Version 2.0

§1 Symptoms
Suspect a reefer failure when the temperature climbs steadily, for example by more than 0.5°C every 10 minutes with the door closed, or when the unit shows an alarm.

§2 Driver actions
The driver stops safely, checks the unit fuel or power, and power-cycles the unit once. The driver reports the result to the dispatcher.

§3 Recovery time limit
If the unit has not restored the setpoint within 15 minutes of the power cycle, the dispatcher redirects the truck to the nearest partner cold-store depot or sends a backup reefer truck for load transfer.

§4 After the event
Fleet Maintenance inspects the unit before it is used again. The truck is marked unavailable in the system until cleared.
